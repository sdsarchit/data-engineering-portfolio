Research Paper Key Summary

Key findings, mathematical methodologies, and model structures from the published NJIT paper.

Abstract & Goal

Geomagnetic storms are primary space weather events that can cause severe damage. Measuring the Disturbance Storm Time (DST) index is crucial for quantifying intensity.

This research performs a rigorous Feature Importance Analysis of the DST Transformer model—a state-of-the-art neural architecture combining Multi-Head Attention and Bayesian Deep learning.

XAI Methodology

  • Kernel SHAP: Generates Shapley values based on cooperative game theory to measure average margin contribution of solar features.
  • LIME Tabular Explainer: Approximates the black-box DST Transformer model locally around specific instances to explain forecasts.
  • Attention Score Maps: Extracts raw weight vectors from the Multi-Head Attention layer to visualize correlation grids.

Key Insights

- Scalar B (IMF) is mathematically proven as the single most critical feature driving DST forecasting.

- BZ GSE exhibits a massive negative correlation (-0.9556) with Scalar B. Southward tilting triggers storms.

- Plasma Temp and Speed are strong supporting indicators. Density and pressure can be safely pruned to reduce complexity.
